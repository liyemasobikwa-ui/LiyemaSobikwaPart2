📏 Wig Size Guide

Getting the perfect fit is essential for comfort and a natural look. Here's how to measure your head:

Circumference:

Measure around your hairline, behind your ears, and to the nape of your neck.

Front to Nape:

Measure from your front hairline to the nape of your neck.

Ear to Ear:

Measure across your head from the top of one ear to the other.

Standard sizes: Petite (21"), Average (22"), Large (23")
