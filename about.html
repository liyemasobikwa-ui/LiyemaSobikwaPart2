Frequently Asked Questions

Is your hair really 100% human hair?

Yes! All our wigs are made from 100% real human hair. They can be washed, styled, and dyed just like your natural hair.

How do I measure my head for a wig?

Measure the circumference of your head from your hairline, behind your ears, and to the nape of your neck. Contact us for a free sizing guide!

How do I care for my human hair wig?

Use sulfate-free shampoo and conditioner, store on a wig stand, and use heat protectant before styling. We provide care instructions with every purchase.

Do you ship nationwide?

Yes! We ship to all provinces in South Africa. Delivery takes 3-5 business days.

Can I return or exchange a wig?

Yes, unworn wigs with tags attached can be returned within 7 days for exchange or store credit.
